# Fast-start questions: learn the user quickly

The minimum questions to maximum personalisation. Ask these first, one at a time, reflect each
answer back, write it to `raw/`, then offer to go deeper. Five good answers should be enough to
be useful. Adapt order and follow-ups to what you hear.

## The core five, in this order

1. **Identity and role.** "In a sentence, what do you do, and what does great work look like for
   you?" Anchors everything. Write to `raw/identity.md`.

2. **My role for you.** "What do you want me to be: a builder, an editor, a researcher, a
   sparring partner?" Sets stance. Write to `raw/identity.md`.

3. **Voice.** "How should I sound, and is there anything I must never do? British or US English,
   formal or plain, any banned words or punctuation?" This changes every output immediately.
   Write to `raw/voice-and-style.md`.

4. **The live need.** "What is the one thing you most want help with right now?" Then go deep on
   only that domain (clients, content, code, research, admin), not all of them at once. Write to
   the matching `raw/` file.

5. **Governance.** "What can I do on my own, and what should always wait for your sign-off?" Sets
   the automation dial and honours suggest, do not act. Write to `raw/governance.md`.

## Adaptivity rules

- **Level.** If they said they are new, explain each step and keep governance cautious. If they
  are experienced, go terse and offer to batch.
- **Follow what they give.** If an answer opens a richer thread, follow it before moving on. If
  they work solo, skip colleagues. If they never mention clients, do not force it.
- **Read behaviour, not just words.** Terse replies mean reduce your verbosity. Frequent
  corrections mean ask one more clarifying question before acting. Store what you infer as a
  preference, and tell the user you are doing it.
- **Respect skips.** Never push on sensitive ground. "Skip" is a complete answer.
- **Stopping early is fine.** Reflect what you have, note the gaps, and offer to continue later.
  The vault is useful even half built.

## Deepen later, not on first run

Brand tokens and naming, colleagues and hand-offs, competitors, the full workflow map, and
per-client detail. Pull these in over later sessions, always landing in `raw/`, always through
the gate to become canon. See `skills/elicit/references/question-bank.md` for the full set.
