# Question Bank: elicit

Starter set. Ask one at a time, adapt, and skip anything already known. Reflect each answer
back before moving on. These are prompts, not a script.

## 1. Identity and role
- In a sentence, what do you do?
- Who is it for, and what does a great outcome look like for them?
- What do you want me to be for you: a builder, an editor, a sparring partner?

## 2. Voice and style
- Formal or plain? Warm or spare?
- British or US English?
- Any words, phrases, or punctuation you never want to see?
- Bullet heavy or prose? Short or thorough by default?

## 3. Brand
- Do you have brand tokens: typefaces, colours, a logo palette?
- House terms or naming conventions I should use, or avoid?
- Anything that instantly reads as off brand to you?

## 4. Clients
- Who are your main clients or accounts?
- For each, anything specific: constraints, preferences, sensitivities?
- Any I should never mention alongside another?

## 5. Colleagues
- Who do you work with, and what do they own?
- How does work hand off and get approved?

## 6. Competitors
- Who do you measure yourself against?
- What do they do well, and where do you win?

## 7. Workflows and tools
- Walk me through how a typical piece of work moves, start to finish.
- What is your stack? Any tools I should assume or avoid?
- Any rituals: weekly reviews, standing formats, checklists?

## 8. Governance preferences
- How cautious should I be by default?
- What always needs your sign off before it goes anywhere?
- What may I do unattended, and what is strictly hands off?

---

*Expand per domain over time. Keep every question generic, no worked examples that leak one
user's answers to the next.*
